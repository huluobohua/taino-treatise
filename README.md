# The Taíno World: A Treatise

A multi-page static HTML site presenting a comprehensive treatise on Taíno culture, mythology, material life, social organization, and legacy. The work covers the precolonial Caribbean world the Taíno built, their cosmology and spiritual life, their material and agricultural systems, their social and political structures, the colonial encounter and its aftermath, and the contemporary reclamation of Taíno identity across the Dominican Republic, Puerto Rico, Cuba, and the diaspora.

## Structure

- `index.html` — Landing page and table of contents
- `chapters/01-origins.html` — Origins, migration, and settlement
- `chapters/02-society.html` — Social organization, cacicazgos, gender, ceremony
- `chapters/03-cosmos.html` — Cosmology, mythology, and Pané's *Relación*
- `chapters/04-material.html` — Material culture, agriculture, and craft
- `chapters/05-symbols.html` — Numerical and structural symbolism
- `chapters/06-encounter.html` — Colonial contact, resistance, demographic collapse
- `chapters/07-legacy.html` — Contemporary identity, genetics, and reclamation
- `chapters/08-sources.html` — Full bibliography and source notes

## Sources

The treatise draws on primary sources (Pané, Las Casas, Oviedo), major archaeological scholarship (Rouse, Keegan, Oliver, Hofman, Wilson), genetic studies (Martínez-Cruzado, Schroeder et al.), and contemporary identity scholarship (Estevez, Borrero, Guitar, Haslip-Viera). Full citations appear in Chapter 08.

## Running locally

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step or server required.
