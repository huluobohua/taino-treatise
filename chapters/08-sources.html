Chapter Eight

Bibliography and Sources

The following bibliography collects the primary sources, secondary scholarship, and institutional references cited throughout this treatise. Entries are organized by chapter, with works cited in multiple chapters listed in full at their first appearance and in abbreviated form thereafter. Primary sources and museum collections are gathered into their own sections.

Primary Sources

Las Casas, Bartolome de. Apologetica Historia Sumaria. Edited by Edmundo O'Gorman. Mexico City: UNAM, 1967. Originally composed mid-16th century.

Las Casas, Bartolome de. Brevisima relacion de la destruccion de las Indias. Seville, 1552.

Fernandez de Oviedo, Gonzalo. Historia General y Natural de las Indias. Seville, 1535; completed posthumously.

Columbus, Christopher. Journal of the First Voyage (Diario). Various editions; the surviving version is Las Casas's abstract.
